Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to common questions about preparing for your appointments, insurance coverage, billing, diagnostic procedures, and patient records.

🔍
How do I schedule or reschedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment directly through our online appointment form, via the secure Patient Portal, or by calling our scheduling desk at +1 (800) 555-0199. To reschedule or cancel, please provide at least 24 hours advance notice to avoid a cancellation fee and allow another patient in need to claim the slot.

Which health insurance providers do you accept?

We are in-network with Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Oxford, and most regional employer-sponsored PPO and POS plans. We also accommodate self-pay patients with clear, upfront discounted fee schedules.

What documents should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport), your current health insurance card, a complete list of all medications and supplements, and any pertinent imaging discs or lab results from outside healthcare systems.

How can I access or request copies of my medical records?

All laboratory test results, clinician consultation notes, and immunization records are available immediately on our 24/7 HIPAA-compliant Patient Portal. If you require certified physical copies or transfers to outside legal/medical representatives, our Medical Records Department processes electronic requests within 48 business hours.

Do you offer virtual telehealth appointments?

Yes, telehealth consultations are available across Primary Care, Cardiology follow-ups, Neurology reviews, and behavioral health. When scheduling online or over the phone, simply select "Telehealth / Virtual Visit" and you will receive a secure video link via SMS and email.

How do I prepare for specialized diagnostic imaging tests?

Preparation varies by procedure: abdominal ultrasounds and fasting blood lipid panels generally require no food or drink for 8-12 hours prior; MRI scans require the removal of all metallic jewelry and verification of any internal pacemakers or metal implants. Detailed preparatory guidelines are emailed 48 hours prior to your scheduled test.

How does patient billing and co-pay collection work?

Copayments and deductible deposits are collected at the time of check-in via credit card, HSA/FSA card, debit card, or check. Any remaining balance after insurance adjudication will be billed electronically with convenient monthly installment payment options available.

Can I request an itemized billing estimate before a procedure?

Yes. Under federal Good Faith Estimate guidelines, our financial counseling team provides clear written estimates for all non-emergency procedures, broken down by physician fee, facility fee, and anticipated anesthesia costs.

What are your visitor guidelines and safety protocols?

Patients visiting outpatient clinics may be accompanied by up to two family members or caregivers. For inpatient and surgical care, visiting hours are from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Hand sanitation stations and HEPA air filtration operate across all clinical wings.

What should I do in the event of an urgent after-hours medical need?

For life-threatening symptoms—including chest pain, severe bleeding, or loss of consciousness—call 911 or visit the nearest emergency room immediately. For non-life-threatening urgent issues after clinic hours, our on-call physician triage service is available 24/7 at +1 (800) 555-0199.
