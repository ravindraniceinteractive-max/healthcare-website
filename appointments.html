Before Your Appointment

To make the most of your consultation, please keep the following in mind:

  • Photo ID & Insurance: Bring a valid government ID and your insurance card for registration verification.
  • Medication List: Bring bottles or a complete list of current prescription and over-the-counter supplements.
  • Past Diagnostic Scans: Bring any recent X-rays, MRI discs, or blood laboratory panels from other clinics.
  • Arrive 15 Minutes Early: Allows ample time for parking and check-in kiosk paperwork.
