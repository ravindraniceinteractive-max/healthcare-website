Cardiovascular Medicine & Heart Care

World-class cardiovascular treatment, non-invasive imaging, arrhythmia therapy, and advanced catheterization procedures designed to protect your heart health.

CLINICAL EXCELLENCE

Advanced Heart Health & Specialized Treatment

At Healthcare, our Cardiology Center of Excellence delivers comprehensive cardiovascular services ranging from preventative lifestyle cardiology to state-of-the-art interventional surgical therapies. Our team of board-certified cardiologists, cardiac electrophysiologists, and cardiovascular surgeons work synchronously to develop precise, individualized care plans.

Whether diagnosing an irregular heartbeat, treating complex coronary artery disease, or guiding cardiac rehabilitation following an intervention, we prioritize minimally invasive methods that promote faster recovery and optimal long-term cardiovascular resilience.

Our Advanced Treatment & Diagnostic Options

We offer fully equipped catheterization laboratories and accredited echocardiography suites to provide accurate diagnostic insights and gentle, effective therapies:

1

Non-Invasive Diagnostic Imaging

Comprehensive echocardiograms, 24-48 hour Holter telemetry monitoring, cardiac CT calcium scoring, and treadmill exercise stress tests.

2

Interventional Cardiology

Transradial catheterization, coronary angioplasty, bioresorbable stent placements, and intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) evaluations.

3

Electrophysiology & Pacemakers

Radiofrequency ablation for arrhythmias, implantation of leadless pacemakers, and subcutaneous defibrillators (ICDs).

4

Monitored Cardiac Rehabilitation

Supervised exercise sessions, personalized nutritional counsel, and lifestyle coaching designed to rebuild cardiovascular stamina safely.

What to Expect During Your Initial Visit

Your initial cardiovascular consultation lasts approximately 45 to 60 minutes. Our cardiologists will conduct a thorough physical examination, review your personal and familial health history, inspect recent bloodwork, and perform an in-office electrocardiogram (EKG). You will leave with a clear diagnostic summary and a written personalized roadmap.

Cardiology Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my chest pain requires immediate emergency care?

Crushing pressure in the chest radiating to your jaw, neck, back, or left arm—especially when accompanied by cold sweats, dizziness, or shortness of breath—warrants calling 911 immediately. Non-emergency evaluations are suitable for intermittent discomfort or routine risk assessments.

What is the difference between an EKG and an Echocardiogram?

An EKG records the electrical signals and rhythm of your heartbeat through surface electrodes. An echocardiogram is an ultrasound that visualizes the physical chambers, valves, muscle thickness, and pumping efficiency of the heart in real time.

Do I need a referral to schedule a cardiology consultation?

While many insurance plans allow direct self-referral to our cardiologists, certain HMO policies require a referral from your primary care physician. Our scheduling team can check your specific plan requirements when you call.
