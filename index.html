COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for visits, billing, and patient services.

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment online using our interactive booking form, by calling our dedicated patient desk at +1 (800) 555-0199, or via our 24/7 patient portal. Same-day appointments are available for urgent consultations.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept major national and regional health insurance providers including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, Medicare, UnitedHealthcare, and Humana. Our billing team will verify your benefits prior to your appointment.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a government-issued photo ID, your active insurance card, a list of current medications and supplements, and any pertinent previous medical records or diagnostic scans from external providers.

How can I access my medical records?

Medical records are accessible 24/7 through our secure HIPAA-compliant Patient Portal. You can view lab results, physician notes, and immunization records, or request formal physical copies through our medical records office.

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes, virtual consultations are offered across primary care, follow-up cardiology visits, mental health, and prescription reviews. You can connect securely from any smartphone, tablet, or computer.

How do I prepare for a diagnostic test?

Preparation varies depending on whether you are undergoing bloodwork (which may require 8-12 hours fasting), ultrasound, MRI, or CT scans. Our clinical team sends personalized prep instructions 48 hours prior to your scheduled test.
