Biography

Dr. Sarah Mitchell earned her medical doctorate from Harvard Medical School and completed her residency in internal medicine at Massachusetts General Hospital. She subsequently completed a four-year clinical and research fellowship in cardiovascular disease at Johns Hopkins Hospital, where she served as Chief Cardiology Fellow.

Prior to joining Healthcare as Chief of Cardiology in 2014, Dr. Mitchell authored over 30 peer-reviewed articles on early coronary artery disease screening and high-resolution echocardiography. She is recognized nationally for her patient-centered approach to cholesterol optimization and cardiac rehabilitation.
